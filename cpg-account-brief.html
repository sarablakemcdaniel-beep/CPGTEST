🏛 What They Do

Church Pension Group is a financial services organization that provides pension, benefits, insurance, and publishing services exclusively for the clergy, lay employees, and institutions of the Episcopal Church.

CPG manages the Church Pension Fund — an $18B+ defined benefit pension fund — alongside group life and health insurance, property & casualty insurance (Church Insurance), and denominational publishing (Church Publishing Inc.).

Their members are Episcopal clergy and lay workers across ~6,500 congregations in the US and internationally. As a regulated financial institution with fiduciary obligations, data quality, compliance, and member-facing digital experience are core priorities.
